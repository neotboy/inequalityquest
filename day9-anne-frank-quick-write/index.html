Day 9 · Anne Frank’s Diary

Read: Friday, October 9, 1942, in your copy of The Diary of a Young Girl. (In the 1969 edition: pages 35–36.)

Before you read: Anne’s diary describes Nazi persecution and the danger faced by Jewish people. The assigned pages refer to deportation, death, and gassing. Other parts of the diary may include executions or hangings, hateful antisemitic language, and upsetting descriptions. This assignment page has no graphic images. Take a moment or speak with your teacher if you need to.

One-page response

Write one paragraph of 5–7 sentences. How does this diary entry show what Jewish people faced under Nazi rule? Use two details from the entry and explain what each detail helps you understand.

Keep your work on this page. You do not need to complete a separate worksheet.
