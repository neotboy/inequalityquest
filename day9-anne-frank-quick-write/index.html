Anne Frank’s Diary · Day 9

Read one line. Explain what it tells you.

Read four pages from Anne’s diary

Anne Frank was a Jewish teenager hiding with her family from the Nazis. This diary entry is dated October 9, 1942. Anne writes about Jewish people being deported and frightening news reaching her family in hiding.

Read Anne’s entry dated Friday, October 9, 1942. In the 1969 edition, it is on pages 35–36. Page numbers can differ in other editions. As you read, notice what Anne has heard about deportations and how she reacts.

Open your class or library copy and read pages 157–160. If your edition has different page numbers, find the entry dated Friday, October 9, 1942.

“Our many Jewish friends are being taken away by the dozen.”

This line is from the October 9, 1942 entry in The Diary of a Young Girl, translated by B. M. Mooyaart-Doubleday.

Write 2–3 sentences

What did Anne learn about what was happening to Jewish people, and how did she react? Use one detail from the entry. Use at least one detail from pages 157–160.

Sentence starter: Anne learned that ___. She reacted by ___. One detail from the diary is ___.
