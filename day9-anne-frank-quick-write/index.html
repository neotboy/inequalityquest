Anne Frank was a Jewish teenager hiding with her family from the Nazis. In a diary entry from March 1944, she wrote about having her own thoughts and opinions.

“I know what I want, I know who’s right and who’s wrong.”

Excerpt from Anne Frank’s diary, 17 March 1944 · Source: Anne Frank House
