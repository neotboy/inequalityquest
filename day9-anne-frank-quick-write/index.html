World History · Day 9

Anne Frank Diary Response

Read one entry. Write 2–3 sentences. Turn it in.

Anne Frank was a Jewish teenager hiding from the Nazis. In this diary entry, she writes about Jewish people being deported—forced from their homes and sent away.

1. Read

In your copy of The Diary of a Young Girl, find the entry dated Friday, October 9, 1942 and read it.

In the 1969 edition, it is on pages 35–36. Page numbers vary by edition, so use the date if yours is different.

2. Write 2–3 sentences

What does Anne’s entry show about what Jewish people were facing? Include one detail from the entry.

You can start: “Anne’s entry shows that Jewish people ___. One detail is ___.”

3. Turn it in

Use your physical or class copy of the book.
