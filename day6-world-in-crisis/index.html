1. Russia: hunger and land

During World War I, many Russian soldiers died. At home, people faced food shortages. Many farmers wanted land. The Tsar, Russia's ruler, lost support. In 1917, protests and a revolution ended his rule.

Clue: People lacked food, wanted land, and no longer trusted the Tsar.

2. China: the emperor loses power

China's last emperor lost power in 1911. After that, different groups fought to control the country. Some people wanted one strong government. Others wanted more local control and changes.

Clue: Leaders fought, and people disagreed about who should rule.

3. India: a call to rule itself

India was ruled by Britain. Many people wanted Indians to make their own laws. Some protested without violence. They asked Britain to give India more control.

Clue: People wanted to make their own laws instead of being ruled by Britain.

4. Southwest Asia: new borders

After World War I, Britain and France took control of areas that had been part of the Ottoman Empire. They drew new borders. Many local people wanted to choose their own leaders.

Clue: Foreign countries set the borders and chose who would rule.

5. The Great Depression: hard times in Germany

In 1929, the stock market crashed in the United States. The economic crisis spread around the world. During the Great Depression, banks and businesses failed, millions of people lost their jobs, and many families struggled to afford food and housing. Governments faced pressure to help people. In Germany, the crisis made many people lose faith in their government. Adolf Hitler and the Nazi Party promised jobs and a stronger Germany, helping them gain support. But the Nazis also blamed and persecuted Jewish people.
