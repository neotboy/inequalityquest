HISTORY BRIEFING · OUTSIDE THE GAME

Real people. Real harm.

The standard also addresses crimes against humanity committed by communist regimes since 1917. These historical events are not game choices or rewards.

Soviet Union under Stalin

During the Great Terror of the 1930s, the regime arrested and executed people and sent many to forced-labor camps. People could be punished as supposed enemies of the state.

Source: Library of Congress ↗

Cambodia, 1975–1979

The communist Khmer Rouge regime forced people from cities, imposed forced labor, persecuted groups, and carried out mass executions. Its policies caused immense suffering and death.

Source: U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum ↗

A regime’s claims and its actual actions must be examined separately. Compare who held power, how opposition was treated, and what happened to people.
