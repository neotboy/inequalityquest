HOW TO PLAY

Booth duty at the Iowa State Fair

Your summer job: work a political party's booth. Your boss picks the booth. Halfway through, you switch to the other party's booth.

  1. Listen. Spot what shaped the fairgoer's beliefs.
  2. Map it. Put them on the idea map.
  3. Pitch. Play the card from your hand that fits them. If your party isn't a fit, be honest and send them to the other booth.

Faster = more corn 🌽. Beat your classmates. The fairgoers are made up. The fair, the booths, and the party positions are real.
