HOW TO PLAY

Win District 101.

  1. Pick your side on 6 hot takes. That's your platform.
  2. Pick a party (or run without one).
  3. Campaign 5 weeks. Click a neighborhood, then use your 3 moves: knock doors, hold an event, or run an ad.
  4. Handle the drama each week. Every choice costs something.
  5. Election night. Win the most votes. Then write 2 short answers and turn in.

No single neighborhood has enough votes. You need several of them on your side. Once a week, click a classmate in the Class Race bar to steal a donor or team up. Polls are game estimates, not real data.
