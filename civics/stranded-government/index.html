HOW TO PLAY

Keep 30 people alive and free.

  1. Go to the gold marker. Click Go to mission and you’ll walk there.
  2. Make the call. Every choice helps something and hurts something.
  3. Watch your meters. Low meters set off surprise events.
  4. Watch Ren. If Ren’s power hits 75, Ren can take over.

Meters: Supplies = food and materials. Safety = order and protection. Trust = people’s willingness to work together. Freedom = rights people keep. These are game scores, not facts about real governments.

Endings: Thriving Republic, Fragile Peace, Revolution, Collapse, or Dictatorship. Your score comes from your meters, your ending, and how many questions you get right on the first try. Guessing costs you points.
