YOUR MISSION

Turn a camp into a government.

The problem: 30 people need water, food, safety, and a way to settle fights. Nobody has agreed on who makes the rules.

  1. Make a public policy. Decide what your government will do about shared needs.
  2. Use the three powers. Write a rule, carry it out, then settle a dispute about it.
  3. Build a state. Connect people, territory, government, and sovereignty.
  4. Put the Preamble to work. Repair water, unite camps, protect the coast, and protect freedom.

Play: Click Go to mission to walk there. Or explore with WASD / arrow keys. At the gold marker, use Investigate. Build, move, or match the items shown.

Your meters: Supplies = materials for public policies. Safety = protection and order. Trust = people’s willingness to cooperate. Freedom = freedoms people keep. These are game scores, not facts about how all governments work.

Win: Finish the government blueprint and keep all four meters above zero. Green supply crates help you carry out your policies.
