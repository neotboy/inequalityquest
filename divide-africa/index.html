FIRST: WHO CONTROLLED WHAT?

Read these short facts. Use them to solve the six questions below.

BRITAIN

Controlled Egypt to protect the Suez Canal. It also controlled Nigeria and South Africa, where trade, gold, and diamonds mattered.

FRANCE

Controlled Algeria in North Africa and sent French settlers there.

BELGIUM

Controlled the Congo in Central Africa and forced many people to collect rubber.

INDEPENDENT

Ethiopia defeated an Italian invasion in 1896. It kept its own government instead of becoming an Italian colony.
