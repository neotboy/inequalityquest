STEP 1: READ — WHO CONTROLLED AFRICA BY 1914?

Read all seven cards. You will need them to solve the mystery places below. They disappear in Part 2.

BRITAIN

Wanted a short sea route to its colony (a land ruled by another country) in India, so it took over Egypt and the Suez Canal (a shortcut for ships between Europe and Asia). It also ruled Nigeria, where British companies bought palm oil, and South Africa, where miners dug gold and diamonds.

FRANCE

Ruled the most land in Africa, mostly in the north and west. In Algeria, hundreds of thousands of European settlers moved in and took farmland from Algerians. French leaders claimed they were "civilizing" Africans — a racist idea that French ways were better.

BELGIUM

King Leopold II owned the Congo as his personal property. People were forced to collect rubber and were beaten or killed if they fell short. In 1908, Belgium's government took over.

GERMANY

Wanted colonies to prove it was a world power. In German East Africa (today's Tanzania), it forced farmers to grow cotton. People fought back in the Maji Maji uprising (rebellion) of 1905.

ITALY

Tried to conquer Ethiopia and lost. Then, in 1911, Italy attacked the Ottoman Empire (the Turkish empire) and took Libya, a desert land just across the sea from Italy.

PORTUGAL

Arrived in Angola in the 1500s and shipped enslaved Africans across the Atlantic. By 1914 it controlled most of Angola and forced many people to work for little or no pay.

INDEPENDENT

Only two African countries stayed free. Ethiopia's army beat Italy at the Battle of Adwa in 1896. Liberia, settled by freed Black Americans, kept its own government.
