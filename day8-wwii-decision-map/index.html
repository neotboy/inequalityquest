Make four decisions

1. Germany attacks Poland · September 1939

Germany invaded Poland. Britain and France had promised to protect Poland.

History: Britain and France declared war on Germany on September 3. They could not stop Poland from being conquered. The war had begun in Europe.

2. France falls · June 1940

Germany used fast attacks with planes, tanks, and soldiers to defeat France. Britain now faced a possible German invasion.

History: Britain's new prime minister, Winston Churchill, chose to keep fighting. Germany bombed Britain, but could not force it to surrender.

3. Japan expands in Asia · 1937–1941

Japan invaded parts of China and moved into Southeast Asia. The United States wanted Japan to stop expanding, but the countries were not at war yet.

History: The United States froze Japanese money and stopped oil sales. The U.S. also kept negotiating. Japan attacked Pearl Harbor after talks failed.

4. Japan attacks Pearl Harbor · December 7, 1941

Japanese planes surprised the U.S. Navy in Hawaii. Ships were damaged or destroyed, and more than 2,400 Americans were killed.

History: The U.S. Congress declared war on Japan the next day. Germany and Italy then declared war on the United States. The U.S. fought in both Europe and the Pacific.
