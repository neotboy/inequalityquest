Content note: This memoir includes hatred of Jewish people, threats, violence, forced work, and deportation. If something upsets you, pause and talk with your teacher.
Book context: Maus I is Art Spiegelman's graphic memoir. Art asks his father, Vladek, to tell what happened to him and his family during Nazi rule. These pages give an overview of the story from that first talk through Vladek and his wife Anja arriving at Auschwitz. The first book ends there, so the pages do not show the rest of their story. The class slides and videos explain how Hitler and Mussolini gained power and how Stalin ruled.
