Before the spark

Why was Europe tense?

4 causes
Nationalism

Strong pride in a nation or people. Some groups wanted their own country.

Militarism

Countries built large armies and navies and prepared for war.

Alliances

Countries promised help to partners. A local fight could pull in allies.

Imperialism

Countries competed for colonies, land, trade, and power.

Remember: The assassination started the July Crisis. The deeper causes had been building for years.

Dispatch complete

The chain reaction reached the world.

One assassination did not cause the war by itself. Years of tension made the crisis dangerous, and each choice brought more countries into the fight.

What to remember
Austria-Hungary’s war with Serbia drew in Russia. Germany backed Austria-Hungary and fought Russia and France. Germany’s invasion of Belgium helped bring Britain in. Empires, colonies, Japan, the Ottoman Empire, and later the United States made the war global.
Long-term causes

Nationalism, militarism, alliances, and imperialism increased tension.

Immediate spark

Archduke Franz Ferdinand was assassinated in Sarajevo on June 28, 1914.

Alliance chain

Support for partners and military plans helped turn the crisis into a wider war.
