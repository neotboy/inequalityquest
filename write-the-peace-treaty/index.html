World War I • 1919

Make a Peace Plan

Choose rules. Write why your team chose them.

What is happening?

World War I is over. France, Britain, and the United States are writing rules for Germany. These rules are called a peace treaty. Germany lost the war. It was not invited to help write the real treaty.

Your goal: Make rules that help stop another war. Try to be fair, too.
1. Read A and B.
2. Pick one plan.
3. Write why you picked it.
Example: We picked A because a smaller army may help keep France safe.
War clue: New weapons made fighting worse. People at home also faced food shortages and worked to support the war.

Choose and write

1. Germany's army

France feared another attack.

2. Paying for damage

The war damaged homes, farms, and towns.

3. Changing borders

A border is a line between countries. Some people wanted a country of their own.

4. Stopping another war

The leaders planned a group called the League of Nations. Countries in the League could meet when there was a problem.

5. Should Germany help write the rules?

Now check the real treaty

Open after you finish all five answers
  • Germany had a small army. It could not have planes or submarines.
  • Germany had to pay money to help rebuild.
  • Germany lost land.
  • The League of Nations was created. Germany could not join at first. The United States did not join.
  • Germany did not help write the treaty. Many Germans felt the rules were unfair. Anger about the treaty later helped Hitler, but it was not the only cause of World War II.
