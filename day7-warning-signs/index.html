Four quick cases

For each case, choose one warning sign. Then enter your name and turn it in. No writing is required.

1. Mussolini: make one leader look great

After World War I, Mussolini promised order and a stronger Italy. His followers attacked rivals. The king made him prime minister in 1922. Mussolini later banned rival parties.

Look at the picture: How is Mussolini shown?

2. Hitler: control what people read

The Nazis burned books by writers they disliked. Nazi leaders also blamed Jewish people for Germany's problems. That was a lie used to turn people against a group.

Look at the picture: What is being destroyed?

3. Stalin: praise the leader, punish critics

Stalin's government used posters and newspapers to praise him. Secret police arrested people called enemies. Some were sent away or killed.

Look at the picture: Who is shown as the hero?

4. Dictators invade other countries

Mussolini's Italy invaded Ethiopia in 1935. Hitler's Germany invaded Poland in 1939. Germany's attack on Poland began World War II in Europe.

Look at the picture: Italian soldiers are marching in Ethiopia.
